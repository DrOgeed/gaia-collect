Riparian & Landscape Restoration

Bringing indigenous trees back to the rivers that need them.

Gaia Collect works with communities to propagate indigenous tree species and restore degraded riverbank ecosystems, one nursery and one planting season at a time.

What We Do

Three pillars, one watershed at a time.

01

Indigenous Tree Propagation

Raising seedlings of locally native species in community-run nurseries, chosen for what actually belongs on that riverbank rather than what grows fastest.

02

Riparian Restoration

Replanting and stabilizing degraded riverbank buffers, protecting water quality and reducing erosion along the corridors that need it most.

03

Community Education

Working alongside the people who live closest to these ecosystems, so restoration outlasts any single planting season.

How We Work

From degraded riverbank to standing canopy.

  • Identify the site

    Walk the riverbank with the community to map degraded or at-risk sections and understand what caused the damage.

  • Raise the right seedlings

    Propagate indigenous species in a community nursery, matched to the specific stretch of river being restored.

  • Plant with the community

    Planting is done together, not delivered to a site, so the people maintaining it understand why each tree is where it is.

  • Monitor and learn

    Track survival over subsequent seasons and feed what's learned back into the next site, honestly, including what didn't take.
